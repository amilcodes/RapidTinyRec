RapidTinyRec Speedrun

How fast can an LLM generative recommender decode, and is beam search even the right algorithm? Batch-1 latency on the frozen 500-user Office cohort; quality on all 4,866 Office test users.

Findings so far

  • A whole MiniOneRec request (prefill, constrained beam, forced-suffix scoring, sort) runs as one CUDA graph in a hand-written engine: 27.9 ms in BF16 with HR@10 identical to the FP32 reference.
  • Certified exact decoding replaces beam search: two forwards score every first code and every two-code prefix, then expand the best-bounded candidates, with a proof that the returned top-K is the global optimum.
  • With trie-renormalized scoring the exact decoder runs in 16.1 ms and certifies its top-50 for 99.9% of users, while beating the FP32 reference on HR@1, HR@10, NDCG@10 and HR@50.
  • MiniOneRec's forced newline/EOS terms cost a forward and slightly hurt ranking; dropping them improves NDCG@10 by +0.36 pt (95% CI +0.13 to +0.61).
